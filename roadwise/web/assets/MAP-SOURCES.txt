Roadwise geographic context
==========================

The offline vector basemap contains generalized geographic reference data.
Highways, urban areas, and county boundaries are concentrated around the
Dallas–Oklahoma City demonstration. Route geometry and weather overlays come
from the planning response. The optional online street layer uses OpenStreetMap
raster tiles and displays its attribution on the map.

Boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundaries as distributed by
us-atlas 3.0.1. See ATLAS-LICENSE.txt.
https://github.com/topojson/us-atlas

Highways: U.S. Census Bureau TIGER/Line 2024 Primary Roads, generalized from
the source geometry. U.S. Government public domain.
https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2024/PRIMARYROADS/

Rivers, lakes, urban areas and populated places: Natural Earth 1:10m, public
domain. Some city labels along the sample corridor were added separately.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Offline JavaScript: React and React DOM 18.3.1 (REACT-LICENSE.txt);
Leaflet 1.9.4 (LEAFLET-LICENSE.txt). DM Sans is distributed under the SIL
Open Font License (FONT-LICENSE.txt).
